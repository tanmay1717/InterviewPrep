Interview Prep Tracker - Settings

Daily reminder

Uses your computer's local time and Chrome notifications. Chrome must be running (it can be in the background) for the alarm to fire.

Gmail auto-detection

Auto-detection is heuristic - it guesses the company from the sender and subject line, and every detected entry lands in the Applications tab as pending review until you confirm or dismiss it. Nothing is ever added to your tracker silently.

Reset

Clears all applications, study plan progress, and settings stored by this extension. This cannot be undone.
